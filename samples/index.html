Specified, not yet built

  • Treemap treemap — Nested rectangles sized by descendant count. Squarified layout.
  • Hive Plot hive — One straight axis per family line; links arc between axes. Endogamy becomes obvious and measurable.
  • Layered Network sugiyama — Proper layered graph drawing with crossing minimisation. The only design that handles heavy cousin marriage without spaghetti.
  • River of Descent sankey — Ribbons whose width is the number of descendants flowing forward through time. Lines that die out visibly narrow to nothing.
  • Map View geo_map — People plotted at their birthplace on a real coastline, with descent lines showing how the family migrated.
  • Star Chart constellation — Force-directed positions drawn as a night sky: people are stars, lineages are constellations, brightness is descendant count.
