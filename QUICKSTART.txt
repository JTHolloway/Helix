HELIX — QUICK START
===================

  1.  Unzip this folder somewhere sensible.

  2.  Open a terminal in it and run ONE command:

          python3 bootstrap.py

      (On Windows you can double-click run.bat instead.)

      That is it. Nothing is installed, nothing is downloaded. In about six
      seconds you will have a sample family, every design rendered to both
      SVG and PDF, and a web page showing them side by side.

  3.  Look at the results:

          samples/index.html      every design, side by side
          samples/pdf/            print-ready, at true physical size
          samples/svg/            for the laser cutter

  4.  Open the actual app:

          python3 -m helix.cli serve sample-family.helix

      Your browser opens. Pick a design from the gallery on the left.

  5.  IF A CHART LOOKS TOO BUSY

      It is showing too many people, not drawing them badly. Try:

          --focus bloodline         everyone you're related to (~170)
          --focus thread_siblings   your line plus siblings    (~45)
          --focus thread            your direct line only      (~20)
          --focus all               every person in the file   (400+)
          --max-generations 4       fewer rings

      Helix always tells you how many names would not fit.

  6.  When you want your own family instead of the sample:

          python3 -m helix.cli init my-family.helix --title "The Whitcombes"
          python3 -m helix.cli serve my-family.helix


NO INTERNET IS USED. NOTHING LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER.
